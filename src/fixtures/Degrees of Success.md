# Degrees of Success & Failure (reworded)

| Result                                    | Attack                                                                      | Evade          | Non-Combat         |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------ |
| **Natural 20 (Critical Hit)**             | Attack crit                                                                 | Evade crit     | Non-combat crit    |
| **Beat target by 10+ (Amazing Success)**  | Attack amazing                                                              | Evade amazing  | Non-combat amazing |
| **Beat target by 0–9 (Standard Success)** | It works, no bonus                                                          | It works       | It works           |
| **Miss by 1–2 (Near Miss)**               | GM calls a near miss, inviting other players to use an Intervene Action     | —              | Near miss          |
| **Miss by 3–9 (Standard Fail)**           | You miss                                                                    | You're hit     | Fail               |
| **Miss by 10+ (Major Fail)**              | Attack major fail                                                           | Evade major    | Non-combat major   |
| **Natural 1 (Critical Fail)**             | Attack crit fail                                                            | Evade crit fail| Non-combat worst   |
